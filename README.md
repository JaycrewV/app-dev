# The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)

## Love:
The **Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)** isn't just a single movie—it's an interconnected storytelling phenomenon. Watching characters evolve across dozens of films and seeing their storylines collide in massive crossover events makes it an unmatched cinematic experience.

## Top 3
1. **Avengers: Endgame (2019)** – The ultimate payoff to over a decade of storytelling.
2. **Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)** – A grounded, high-stakes political thriller with incredible action choreography.
3. **Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)** – Proof that even obscure comic characters can become iconic with the right heart, humor, and soundtrack.

> *"Part of the journey is the end."* — Tony Stark.
